# Product Mission

> Last Updated: 2025-01-26
> Version: 1.0.0

## Pitch

Bitcoin Simulation Tool is a comprehensive financial simulation platform that helps Bitcoin holders and aspiring investors understand and optimize Bitcoin-backed loan strategies by providing advanced price prediction models and investment strategy simulations to accumulate more Bitcoin or generate sustainable income from their holdings.

## Users

### Primary Customers

- **Bitcoin Holders (20-80 years)**: Individuals who own Bitcoin and want to leverage it as collateral without selling, regardless of their technical knowledge about bullet loans or leverage
- **Income Seekers**: People looking to generate sustainable monthly income from their Bitcoin holdings without depleting their stack
- **Bitcoin Accumulators**: Investors who want to use rolling loan strategies to acquire more Bitcoin over time

### User Personas

**The Bitcoin Retiree** (50-70 years old)
- **Role:** Retiree or pre-retiree
- **Context:** Has accumulated significant Bitcoin holdings and wants to live off them without selling
- **Pain Points:** Fear of selling Bitcoin too early, lack of understanding of loan mechanics, uncertainty about sustainable withdrawal rates
- **Goals:** Generate predictable monthly income, preserve Bitcoin holdings, understand risk levels

**The Bitcoin Accumulator** (25-45 years old)
- **Role:** Professional investor or high earner
- **Context:** Wants to maximize Bitcoin accumulation using leverage strategies
- **Pain Points:** Complex loan calculations, timing market cycles, managing liquidation risks
- **Goals:** Accumulate more Bitcoin efficiently, optimize leverage strategies, time market cycles

**The Financial Advisor** (30-60 years old)
- **Role:** Financial planner or investment advisor
- **Context:** Needs to advise clients on Bitcoin-backed strategies
- **Pain Points:** Lack of tools for Bitcoin strategy analysis, difficulty explaining complex concepts to clients
- **Goals:** Provide data-driven advice, demonstrate strategy outcomes, educate clients

## The Problem

### Lack of Bitcoin Strategy Simulation Tools

While various Bitcoin price prediction models exist (Power Law, Stock-to-Flow, Quantile Models), there are no comprehensive tools that simulate different strategies for using Bitcoin as collateral. This leaves investors making critical financial decisions without proper analysis.

**Our Solution:** Combine multiple price prediction models with strategy simulation engines to provide comprehensive Bitcoin-backed loan analysis.

### Complex Loan Mechanics Without Guidance

Most Bitcoin holders don't understand bullet loans, leverage ratios, or liquidation risks, making it dangerous to use Bitcoin as collateral without proper planning and risk assessment.

**Our Solution:** Provide intuitive interfaces and educational content that make complex financial concepts accessible to users of all technical levels.

### No Long-term Strategy Optimization

Existing tools focus on current market conditions but don't help users plan long-term strategies across different market cycles and economic scenarios.

**Our Solution:** Simulate strategies across multiple years and market cycles using historical data and various price projection models.

## Differentiators

### Comprehensive Price Model Integration

Unlike standalone price prediction tools, we integrate multiple proven models (Power Law, Stock-to-Flow, Cycle Repeat, Manual Growth) with actual strategy simulation. This provides realistic projections based on different market scenarios rather than just price predictions.

### Strategy-Focused Approach

Unlike general DeFi calculators, we specifically focus on Bitcoin-backed loan strategies with detailed risk management, liquidation scenarios, and optimization for both accumulation and income generation goals.

### Educational Accessibility

Unlike complex financial tools that require expertise, we make advanced Bitcoin strategies accessible to users aged 20-80 with varying technical knowledge through intuitive interfaces and comprehensive educational content.

## Key Features

### Core Features

- **Modular Price Projection System:** Independent microservices for Power Law, Stock-to-Flow, Quantile Model, Cycle Repeat, and Manual Growth Rate projections
- **Strategy Simulation Engine:** ATH-Based, Moving Average, and ATH Collateral strategies as independent microservices with customizable parameters
- **Historical Data Analysis:** Bitcoin price data since 2013 with support/resistance line calculations and real-time API integration
- **Risk Management Tools:** LTV monitoring, liquidation warnings, and safety margin calculations across all models and strategies
- **Interactive Charts:** Historical data visualization and price projection charts with multiple scenarios and model comparisons

### Strategy Features

- **Income Generation Mode:** Simulate sustainable monthly withdrawals from Bitcoin collateral using any price model
- **Accumulation Mode:** Optimize rolling loan strategies to acquire more Bitcoin over time with model-specific projections
- **Risk Assessment:** Detailed analysis of liquidation risks and safety margins across different market conditions and price models
- **Scenario Planning:** Compare multiple strategies and price models side-by-side with confidence scoring
- **Model Integration:** Seamless integration between price projection microservices and strategy simulation engines

### Educational Features

- **Strategy Explanations:** Clear documentation of how each strategy works and when to use it
- **Risk Education:** Interactive guides explaining bullet loans, leverage, and liquidation mechanics
- **Best Practices:** Recommendations based on market conditions and user goals
